How to use

  1. Open any .html file in your browser.
  2. Fill in the fields on screen.
  3. Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac) → choose your printer or Save as PDF.
  4. Print at 100% scale on US Letter or A4. Turn background graphics off in the print dialog for cleanest results.
