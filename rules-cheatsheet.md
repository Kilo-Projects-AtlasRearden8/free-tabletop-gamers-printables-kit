## Core loop

- Roll `d20` + modifier vs. target number (AC / DC / save DC). Meet or beat = success.
- Advantage: roll two `d20`, take the higher. Disadvantage: take the lower.
- Natural 20 on an attack = critical hit (usually double damage dice). Natural 1 = automatic miss.
- Action economy: 1 action, 1 bonus action, 1 reaction, movement up to speed per turn.

Tip: print one cheat-sheet per player and keep it by the dice tray. House rules override this sheet.
